# Relicense to Apache 2.0 (2026-10-06)

**Decision:** Downshift (repository `harness-downshift`) is distributed under
**Apache License, Version 2.0** from commit date **2026-10-06** onward.

**Rationale:** Remove licence friction during beta adoption and community
contributions while keeping a path to commercial products (hosted router,
support, enterprise) under the **Downshift** brand.

**Prior licence:** Business Source License 1.1 — full text archived in
[LICENSE-BSL-1.1-ARCHIVE.md](../LICENSE-BSL-1.1-ARCHIVE.md).

**Contributions:** New contributions are licensed under Apache 2.0 per
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Past contributions under BUSL remain
owned by their authors; the maintainer relicensed the combined work where
permitted.

**Not legal advice.** For your organisation’s compliance review, use
[LICENSE](../LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE).
